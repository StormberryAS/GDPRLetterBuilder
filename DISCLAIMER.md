## Specific to GDPRLetterBuilder

GDPRLetterBuilder gives **information, not legal advice**. It fills in standard letters that a private person can use to exercise their own rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Norwegian Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven). It does not assess your situation, it does not tell you whether a company has broken the law, and using it does not make Stormberry AS your lawyer or adviser.

The letters and the guidance were checked against the sources listed in the app on 12 October 2026. Law and guidance change, and the app is not kept in step with them, so check the current position with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or a lawyer before you rely on it for anything important.

The deadline the app shows is an estimate. It counts from the date you give for when the company received your letter, which the app cannot know and, for post, only estimates; it follows the counting method the European Data Protection Board describes, and it takes only Norwegian public holidays into account. Where necessary because of the complexity or number of requests, a company may extend the deadline by up to two further months, if it tells you so, with its reasons, within the first month. The law does not say exactly how the extra months are counted, so the app shows the later reading.

A company may lawfully refuse a request or keep some of your data, for example when a law requires it to keep records. The app cannot know whether that applies to you.

The app sends nothing and keeps nothing you type. You choose whether to copy, print, save or email the letter, and you send it yourself.

The app is meant for your own requests, one letter at a time. It is not a tool for businesses to meet their own duties under the GDPR.
