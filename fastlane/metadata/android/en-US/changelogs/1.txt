First release, and an early one: 0.0.1 is a functioning prototype, so expect gaps and changes. GDPRLetterBuilder writes a GDPR access or erasure letter in English or Bokmål, counts the company's deadline and hands a reminder to your calendar app, with follow-up letters and a cover note for a complaint to Datatilsynet. Information, not legal advice. No permissions, no internet access; it sends nothing. Full log: https://github.com/StormberryAS/GDPRLetterBuilder/releases
